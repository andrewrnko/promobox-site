How muchis a wrap?

Our real starting prices for vans and trucks, from door lettering to a full wrap, and the five things that move the number.

Plenty of shops say the price comes after a look. We put ours on the page. Here are the starting prices from our price page, what each level gets you, and the things that push a quote up or down.

The starting prices

Vehicle graphics packages start from $349. Past that, our price page lists five levels for vans and five for trucks. The low levels are lettering and spot graphics. The middle levels add printed panels and partial coverage. The top level is a full wrap.

These are starting points, not quotes. Two vans at the same level can land at different prices. Here is why.

What moves the price

  1. 1

    Coverage

    How much of the vehicle gets film. This is the biggest lever by far. A door logo and a full wrap are different jobs.

  2. 2

    Size and shape

    A high-roof Sprinter has more surface than a small pickup. Deep recesses, rivets and bumpers take more install time.

  3. 3

    Design work

    A simple layout from a clean logo goes fast. A custom illustration or a full rebuild of old artwork takes longer.

  4. 4

    Paint condition

    Film follows the surface. Rust, chips and failing paint need attention before anything goes on, and some of it cannot be wrapped over.

  5. 5

    Extras

    DOT numbers, rear door graphics, window perf and reflective markings each add a line.

Shop note

Comparing two quotes? Make sure they cover the same panels, the same design work and the same film before you compare totals. A cheaper quote often covers less of the van.

Spend where the message works

More coverage is not always the better buy. Think about how the vehicle gets seen. Parked at a jobsite all day, the sides do the work. Stuck in traffic on I-5, the rear doors do. Rank the views that matter, then put the money there.

A white van with a big name, one service line and a phone number can carry the basic message for a lot less money. A full wrap earns its price when you want color, photos and a look nobody forgets. Neither one is the wrong answer. They do different jobs.
