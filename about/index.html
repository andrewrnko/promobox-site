About Promo Box

One machine.One garage.

Promo Box started in 2008 in a garage with one secondhand embroidery machine. Now it is a full wrap, apparel and sign shop on Everett Mall Way.

Get a quote →(425) 737-9026

It started in 2008. Andrey Tsarenko bought a secondhand embroidery machine for $4,500 and set it up in a garage. The first real order was 100 hats for $900.

From there the shop added apparel printing, signs, print and vehicle graphics, and moved out of the garage into a real shop. Embroidery is still a whole department.

Today the shop is at 909 SE Everett Mall Way. Design, printing, cutting, embroidery and install all happen under one roof, on our own equipment: HP Latex and Epson wide-format printers, a Colex flatbed cutter, a Mimaki DTF printer and Barudan embroidery machines. In March 2026 the shop added a dedicated clean room, and window tint and paint protection film came back.

We work for local contractors and service crews, for public agencies like South County Fire and the Redmond Police Department, and quietly for other wrap shops and agencies. The rule is the same for all of them. If something isn't right, we fix it.

How it works

Design. Proof.Print. Install.

Step 01

Design

  1. 01

    Design

    Send a logo, photos of the vehicle or a rough idea. Our designers build the layout. Free design proofs on every order.

  2. 02

    Proof

    You get a proof to check. Spelling, phone number, colors, placement. Nothing prints until you approve it.

  3. 03

    Print

    Printed, cut and stitched in our own shop. Standard print turns in 2 business days. Banners 2 to 3, signs 5 to 7.

  4. 04

    Install or pick up

    Our installers put on wraps, window graphics and signs. Print and apparel are ready for pickup in Everett.
